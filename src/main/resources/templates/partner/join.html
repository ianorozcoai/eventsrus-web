Still wondering?

Is it really free?

Yes. You use EventsRUs PRO for free until you get your first 3 bookings, for up to 12 months. A booking counts when a planner books you on EventsRUs and submits their payment in the app.

What if I get no bookings?

We can't promise bookings, so we don't charge you until you've had your first 3. If you don't get them, you can stay on the free period for up to 12 months.

What happens after my 3 bookings?

You choose a plan, and you get a 30-day grace period to decide. Plan prices are shown in the app before you pay anything.

Do you take a cut of my payments?

No. Clients pay you directly through your GCash, Maya, PayPal or bank details. EventsRUs never holds your clients' money.

I'm not good with technology.

If you can use Facebook and GCash, you can do this. Watch the 5-minute video above, or book a live walkthrough with us and we'll show you.

Who are the planners?

Event planners and hosts in the Philippines, organising debuts, weddings, birthdays and corporate events. Planners use EventsRUs for free, always.

Can I stop any time?

Yes. There is no contract to sign.
