Questions suppliers ask.

Is it really free?

Yes. You use EventsRUs PRO for free until you get your first 3 bookings, for up to 12 months. After that you choose a plan. We can't promise bookings, but there is nothing to lose by trying.

Do you take a cut of my payments?

No. Clients pay you directly through the payment details on your storefront, such as GCash, Maya, PayPal or bank transfer. EventsRUs does not handle your clients' money.

What if I don't want to show my prices?

Choose Request for Quotation on any package. Clients send you their event details and you reply with a custom quote. You can mix fixed prices, price ranges and quote-only packages in one storefront.

How do I know who is interested?

Your Leads page shows planners who visited your storefront, with their name, their event and the event date, so you can message them first.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. The website works on any phone browser, and the EventsRUs app is now available for Android on Google Play and for iPhone on the App Store.

How are suppliers verified?

The EventsRUs team reviews the ID and business details you submit before your storefront gets the Verified badge. Verification usually does not stop you from receiving bookings in the meantime.

Can I see it before I sign up?

Yes. Watch the 5-minute demo above, or book a live walkthrough with us.
